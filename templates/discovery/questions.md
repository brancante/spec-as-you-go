# Discovery question bank

Pick by gap, not in order. Use the phrasing that matches the user's depth **in that area** (profile). Never ask what the code or materials already answered.

| Area | Expert phrasing (depth 4–5) | Plain phrasing (depth 1–2) | Mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outcome | What metric moves if this succeeds, and what's the current baseline? | What would be different for you or your customers if this works? | both |
| Users | Which segment has the sharpest pain and the shortest sales cycle? | Who needs this most urgently, and how do they solve it today? | both |
| Results | What do retention and activation look like by cohort? | Do people keep using it after the first weeks? How do you know? | brownfield |
| Feedback | What are the top churn reasons and top feature requests, by frequency? | What do customers complain about most, and what do they ask for most? | brownfield |
| Marketing | Which positioning claims are you making, and which are proven? | What does your website or pitch promise? Is any of it not true yet? | brownfield |
| Manual | Where does onboarding break: which step has the biggest drop-off? | Where do new users get stuck or ask for help? | brownfield |
| Competitors | Who wins deals against you, and on what axis? | When someone picks another option over you, what do they pick and why? | both |
| Differentiation | What's hard to copy here: data, distribution, integration depth? | If a big company copied you tomorrow, what would they still not have? | both |
| Hotspot | `<file>` has high churn and no tests. Known pain, and core to the value? | One part of the system breaks or changes a lot. Is it the part customers care about most? | brownfield |
| Security | Where is PII/payment data, and what are the compliance obligations? | Do you store personal or payment data? Any rules you must follow (LGPD, GDPR, PCI)? | both |
| Scale | Expected load in 12 months, and the latency/cost budget per request? | How many users do you expect in a year? Does it need to feel instant? | both |
| Constraints | Hard deadlines, team size, budget, platform lock-ins? | Is there a date, a budget limit, or a technology you must use? | both |
| Known improvements | What's in the backlog that you'd fix first with a free week? | If you could fix one thing this month, what would it be? | brownfield |
| Why now | What changed (tech, regulation, market) that makes this viable now? | Why is now the right time for this? | greenfield |
